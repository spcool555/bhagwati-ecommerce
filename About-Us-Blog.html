Our Journey: From Humble Beginnings to Industry Leaders

May 24, 2026 Admin Company Story

Bhagwati Cards and Paper Products was founded with a simple vision: to provide high-quality, affordable stationery and paper products to customers across India. What started as a small family business has grown into a trusted name in the industry.

Our journey began more than a decade ago when our founders recognized the need for reliable and quality paper products in the market. With dedication, hard work, and a commitment to excellence, we have expanded our operations to serve thousands of satisfied customers.

Quality First

We never compromise on the quality of our products. Every item is carefully selected and inspected.

Customer Care

Your satisfaction is our priority. We provide excellent customer service and support.

Reliability

We stand behind every product with our 30-day money-back guarantee.

Today, we are proud to serve customers from all walks of life - students, professionals, businesses, and organizations. Our extensive range of products includes cards, notepads, writing supplies, file storage, and much more.

The Values That Drive Us Forward

May 20, 2026 Admin Company Culture

At Bhagwati Cards and Paper Products, we believe that success is built on a foundation of strong values and principles. These values guide everything we do, from product selection to customer service.

Our Core Values:

  • Integrity: We conduct our business with honesty and transparency, building trust with every customer and partner.
  • Innovation: We continuously seek ways to improve our products and services to meet evolving customer needs.
  • Excellence: We strive for excellence in everything we do, maintaining high standards across all operations.
  • Sustainability: We are committed to environmentally responsible practices in sourcing and packaging our products.
  • Community: We believe in giving back to the community and supporting local businesses and causes.

These values are not just words on a wall - they are embedded in our company culture and reflected in our daily operations. Every team member is dedicated to upholding these principles and delivering exceptional value to our customers.
